Mission

Providing individualized assistance so that they may have access to timely and appropriate interventions without unnecessary delays or duplication of services.

Vision

Effective and efficient means of managing the client’s transitions through medical and social services. Clients are assigned to a Patient Navigator to assisting them in the process of entering into medical care and helping to clarify both medical concerns/issues and providing of referrals.

Services

  • Patient transfer assistance – Assist Patient that needs to be transferred whether from hospital to another hospital or from house to hospital transfer.
  • Coordinating with the Department of Health (DOH) Calabarzon for a Patient Navigation and Referral System (PNRS) is a crucial step in enhancing the healthcare services in the region, ensuring seamless care for patients, especially in cases that require specialized treatment, emergency care, or follow-up services.
  • Ambulance Services: Offering emergency transport for patients requiring urgent medical attention to hospitals or health facilities.
  • Answering phone calls regarding question and queries about different services of the City Health Office. (Animal Bite, TB Dots, Sanitation Division, Dental, OPD-Related, Hospital Schedules, Social Hygiene Clinic, Vaccine Availability, Etc.).

The Antipolo Patient Navigation and Referral Unit Workflow

  1. Entry Point: Patient arrives via the Outpatient Department (OPD), Emergency Room (ER), or is referred from an external primary care clinic.
  2. Screening & Intake: The Navigator assesses the patient's clinical needs, insurance status, and social barriers (transportation, language, etc.).
  3. Internal/External Triage:
    • Internal: Directing the patient to a specialist within the same hospital.
    • External: Coordinating a transfer to a higher-level facility or a specialized diagnostic center.
  4. Coordination: The PNRU staff handles the "paperwork"—booking appointments, sending medical records, arranges transportation and securing prior authorizations.
  5. Follow-Up: Ensures patient completes referrals and receives ongoing support.
